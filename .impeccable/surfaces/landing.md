# Landing page

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Qwixit’s landing page is the product mechanism at working scale: selected text becomes corrected text without leaving the current app. It refuses the centered marketing headline above a detached dashboard screenshot.

**OWN-WORLD:** Landing Kit canon: cool macOS paper, near-black ink, real light product surfaces, restrained elevation, electric violet, and small semantic cyan/magenta/green signals with darker accessible text variants on pale backgrounds. System sans handles explanations; Unbounded carries short localized statements; JetBrains Mono carries state, shortcuts, metadata, and controls.

**STORY:** Six blocks, one job each: the visitor sees the one-shortcut replacement, recognizes the copy-paste detour it removes and closes that block with the paired proof “Fast by workflow / Private by design,” understands Fix/Actions/Peek through real product fragments, verifies three concrete privacy facts, sees pricing integrated with the real menu-bar usage panel, and reaches the final macOS call to action. There is no separate native-utility block.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact localized headline and bottom-right-chamfer CTA sit beside a real-scale Notes workspace. Hero and detour share one continuous 4.5-second clock: 1.5 seconds of selected source text, 1.5 seconds of shortcut plus the real light PROCESSING HUD, then 1.5 seconds of corrected replacement plus READY / TEXT REPLACED. The transition never exposes a blank state. This is the signature interaction.

**FORM:** Landing Kit alignment is final canon. Product evidence remains light and faithful to the app: the 236×58 HUD with three processing Face frames, per-letter wave/trace, and a violet→cyan→green success handoff; the 432×260 Actions Palette with 50px header and 38px footer; translated Peek at 440px wide and at most 380px high unpinned, with language tabs, scrolling, word count, Pin, and Copy; and the 318px menu panel labeled QWIXIT 1.4.2 with Cloud, Access, and 12/30 ACTIONS. Punk controls use opposing top-left and bottom-right chamfers, gradient registration edges, a short top dash, and restrained glow. English, Polish, and Ukrainian are first-class layouts. Until a real downloadable build exists, every macOS CTA says the localized equivalent of “macOS — coming soon” and opens the status toast.

**FINISH:** Final review is recorded in `desktop-uk-final.png`, `desktop-pl-final.png`, and `mobile-final.png`. Documentation must match the shipped six-block structure, three-row privacy trace, menu-bar pricing integration, localized copy, and current CTA availability.
