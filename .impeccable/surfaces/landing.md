# Landing page

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** hotkeygo’s landing page is the product mechanism at working scale: selected text becomes corrected text without leaving the current app. It refuses the centered marketing headline above a detached dashboard screenshot.

**OWN-WORLD:** Landing Kit canon: cool macOS paper, near-black ink, real light product surfaces, restrained elevation, electric violet, and small semantic cyan/magenta/green signals with darker accessible text variants on pale backgrounds. System sans handles explanations; Unbounded carries short localized statements; JetBrains Mono carries state, shortcuts, metadata, and controls.

**STORY:** Six blocks, one job each: the visitor sees the one-shortcut replacement, recognizes the copy-paste detour it removes, sees both workflows run in parallel: the measured 3–5 second action range is represented by a four-second HotkeyGo animation while the illustrative chat process continues, with no numeric chat duration shown, and closes that block with the paired proof “Fast by workflow / Private by design,” understands Fix/Actions/Peek through real product fragments, verifies three concrete privacy facts, sees pricing integrated with the real menu-bar usage panel, and reaches the final macOS call to action. There is no separate native-utility block.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact localized headline and bottom-right-chamfer CTA sit beside a real-scale Notes workspace. Hero and detour share one continuous 4.5-second clock: 1.5 seconds of selected source text, 1.5 seconds of shortcut plus the real light PROCESSING HUD, then 1.5 seconds of corrected replacement plus READY / TEXT REPLACED. The transition never exposes a blank state. This is the signature interaction.

**FORM:** Landing Kit alignment is final canon. Product evidence remains light and faithful to the app: the 236×58 HUD with three processing Face frames, per-letter wave/trace, and a violet→cyan→green success handoff; the searchable 360 pt Actions palette scaled to 432 px wide, with four numbered choices and a 32 pt replacement/undo footer; Peek’s read-only result state at 440 × 380 px, with source and target language controls, scrollable translation, and a copy/close footer; and the 318 × 190 px native menu popup with info/version and Cloud, Free usage/shortcut/access status, Preview & Approve off, Settings/Quit, and a full-width Onboarding action. Punk controls use opposing top-left and bottom-right chamfers, gradient registration edges, a short top dash, and restrained glow. English, Polish, and Ukrainian are first-class layouts. Until a real downloadable build exists, every macOS CTA says the localized equivalent of “macOS — coming soon” and opens the status toast.

**FINISH:** The current production build was visually checked in EN/PL/UK at desktop and at 390×844 on mobile. Tracked PNGs in `.impeccable/review/` belong to the earlier Qwixit iteration and are historical; refresh them before using them as current handoff artifacts. Documentation must match the shipped six-block structure, three-row privacy trace, menu-bar pricing integration, localized copy, and current CTA availability.
